# SaaSPulse before/after: agent-DX branch (2026-10-20)

This compares the generation changes on `feat/agent-dx` (grounded schema context, threshold-gated pruning, the expanded repair loop) against the code they replace.

## Setup

- **Before:** PR #25 head (`98ac829`, the validator fixes on top of `main`). This is old prompt, validation-only repair, and 2 attempts.
- **After:** `feat/agent-dx` merged with PR #25. Same validator, so only this branch's changes differ. New prompt, repair on validation, database and empty-result outcomes, and 3 attempts.
- **Model:** Claude Haiku (`claude-haiku-5-5`) via the local `claude` CLI ([`run_with_claude_cli.py`](../run_with_claude_cli.py)): `AnthropicSQLGenerator` with no tools, settings or hooks; 6 workers.
- **Suites:** each has 65 correctness cases with unchanged gold SQL.
  - *Core:* `saaspulse/correctness.yaml`.
  - *Natural:* `saaspulse_natural/natural.yaml`, the same questions in business language.
  - *Wide:* the natural suite against `make_saaspulse_wide.py`, which adds 300 distractor tables (308 in total).

## Results

| Suite | Runs | Before: correct | After: correct | Before: first-pass | After: first-pass | Prompt tokens / question (before → after) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core | 3 | 194/195 (99.5%) | 195/195 (100%) | 194/195 | 194/195 | 426 → 907 |
| Natural | 3 | 195/195 (100%) | 195/195 (100%) | 193/195 | 193/195 | 426 → 907 |
| Wide (308 tables) | 2 | 130/130 (100%) | 130/130 (100%) | 130/130 | 128/130 | 13,323 → 4,884 (−63%) |

Prompt tokens are estimated at 4 characters per token from the rendered prompt; the `claude` CLI doesn't report per-call usage.

## What this shows

- **SaaSPulse is saturated for current small models.** Haiku answers 99.5–100% of cases before and after, even with business phrasing and 300 distractor tables. The suite can't show an accuracy gain for this model. It does show that the branch holds accuracy while cutting large-schema prompts by more than half.
- **The benchmark caught two regressions in this branch before they shipped.** Both are fixed, and the final numbers above include the fixes.
  1. On the wide schema, pruning dropped `accounts` for questions that said "customer", because distractor tables sharing a `segment` column outranked it. This cost 3 cases per run. Join partners of relevant tables now inherit their score, and a "can't answer" on a pruned schema retries with a 4× larger context.
  2. Top-3 sample values for `priority` (`'normal'`, `'low'`, `'high'`) led the model to map "urgent" onto `'high'`. Small enums now list every value ("one of …"). Gold-literal coverage went from 18/22 to 22/22.
- **Offline, without a model** ([`schema_context_report.py`](../schema_context_report.py)): FK lines cover 26/26 gold joins, the gold queries' string literals are 22/22 present in the prompt, and pruning keeps 100% of needed tables and columns down to a 400-token budget on the 8-table schema and at the default budget on the 308-table schema.

## Next

To measure accuracy gains, the benchmark needs headroom: a weaker or local model (`--generator openai-compatible`), messier data (codes rather than words, inconsistent casing), or ambiguous business definitions that need `hints`. These are tracked in [docs/roadmap.md](../../docs/roadmap.md).
